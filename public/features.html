🎮 Features

Discover everything ZooBot has to offer - from character collection to epic battles

Core Gameplay

Character Collection

The heart of ZooBot - catch, collect, and power up your characters

🎯

Character Drops

Characters drop every 30 seconds with unique codes. Be the first to type the code and add them to your collection!

📊

Leveling System

Level up your characters to increase their HP, damage, and unlock powerful abilities. Higher levels mean stronger battles!

🎨

Character Skins

Customize your characters with 5 tiers of rarity skins. Stand out from other players with exclusive cosmetics!

🔑

Key System

Collect keys to unlock rare caged characters. Random cage openings for surprise rewards!

⭐

ST Boosts

Boost your characters' stats with ST (Special Tokens). Each character can be boosted for enhanced performance!

📦

Game Bundles

Unique per-server game selection with custom character pools. Each server can choose their own game theme!

Combat System

Epic Battles

Engage in strategic turn-based combat with 51 unique abilities

⚔️

Turn-Based Combat

Strategic battles where timing and move selection matter. Manage your energy wisely to maximize damage!

💫

51 Unique Abilities

Each character has passive abilities that trigger during battle. From lifesteal to critical boost, master them all!

⚡

Energy Management

Powerful moves cost more energy. Balance between light attacks and devastating special moves!

🏆

Trophy Rankings

Win battles to earn trophies and climb the competitive rankings. Prove you're the best battler!

Economy

Multi-Currency Economy

A deep economy system with 4 currencies and multiple ways to earn

💰

Coins

The primary currency. Earn from drops, battles, work, and daily rewards. Use for leveling and crates!

💎

Gems

Rare premium currency for high-tier crates and special purchases. Harder to obtain but worth the effort!

🏆

Trophies

Earned through PvP battles. Higher trophies mean higher ranks on the competitive leaderboard!

🌟

UST (Universal Skill Tokens)

Exclusive currency from Clan Wars. Purchase premium skins and profile pictures that can't be bought elsewhere!

Community

Social Features

Connect with other players through trading, clans, and more

🔄

Trading System

Secure peer-to-peer trading with dual confirmation. Trade characters, coins, and gems safely!

🏰

Clan System

Join your server's clan, donate resources, and compete in weekly Clan Wars for UST rewards!

🏪

Marketplace

List items for sale and browse what others are selling. A player-driven economy!

🎯

Auctions

Create and bid on auctions for rare items. Compete with other players for the best deals!

📊

Leaderboards

Global and server leaderboards for coins, gems, battles, trophies, and collectors!

🎉

Events & Giveaways

Daily rotating events, lottery system, and automated giveaways to keep things exciting!

Gameplay

Minigames & Activities

Endless ways to earn and have fun beyond collecting

💼

Work System

5 unique jobs: Miner, Caretaker, Farmer, Zookeeper, Ranger. Each with minigames and special rewards!

🎰

Gambling Games

Coin Duel, Dice Clash, Door of Fate, and Almost Win Machine. High risk, high reward!

Trivia System

Quest System

Complete daily and weekly quests for bonus rewards. Track your progress and claim when done!

🎖️

Achievements

Earn badges for milestones: First Trade, Millionaire, Legend status, and more!

📅

Daily Rewards

Claim daily rewards with streak bonuses. 7-day streaks give massive bonus rewards!
