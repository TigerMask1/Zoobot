🦁 About ZooBot

The story behind the ultimate Discord character collection game

Our Mission

ZooBot was created with a simple goal: bring the excitement of character collection, strategic battles, and community competition to Discord servers everywhere.

We believe gaming should be accessible, engaging, and social. That's why ZooBot is designed to foster community interaction through trading, clan wars, and friendly competition.

With 51+ unique characters, a deep battle system with unique abilities, and a robust economy, ZooBot offers an experience that rivals dedicated gaming platforms - all within Discord.
