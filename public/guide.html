📖 Player Guide

Everything you need to know to master ZooBot and dominate the leaderboards

🎮 Getting Started

Welcome to ZooBot! Here's how to begin your character collection journey:

Step 1: Initialize Your Account

Type !start in any channel where ZooBot is active. This creates your player profile and gives you a starter character!

Step 2: Catch Your First Characters

Characters drop every 30 seconds in designated drop channels. When you see a drop, type !c [code] quickly to catch it. The fastest player wins!

Step 3: Build Your Collection

Keep catching drops, open crates, and trade with other players to grow your collection. Use !chars to view your collection anytime.

💰 Currency Guide

💰 Coins

The primary currency used for most activities:

  • Earned from: Drops, battles, work, daily rewards, quests
  • Used for: Leveling characters, buying crates, trading

💎 Gems

Rare premium currency for special purchases:

  • Earned from: Daily rewards, rare drops, events, premium crates
  • Used for: Legendary crates, special items, activating drops

🏆 Trophies

Competitive currency earned through battles:

  • Earned from: Winning PvP battles
  • Used for: Ranking on leaderboards, clan donations

🌟 UST (Universal Skill Tokens)

Exclusive currency from Clan Wars:

  • Earned from: Weekly Clan War rewards (top 3 clans)
  • Used for: Exclusive skins and profile pictures

🎉 Auto Giveaways & Lottery

Participate in daily automated giveaways and lotteries for amazing rewards!

Automatic Giveaways

  • Schedule: Runs automatically at 00:00 UTC every day
  • Duration: 60 minutes
  • Prize: 500 gems to the winner
  • How to Enter: React to the giveaway message when it starts
  • Winner Announcement: Posted in events channel across all servers

Automatic Lottery

  • Schedule: Runs automatically at 00:00 UTC every day
  • Duration: 60 minutes
  • Entry Fee: Configurable by server admins (gems or coins)
  • Prize Pool: All entry fees go to the winner
  • How to Enter: Use !lottery join <tickets>

Commands

  • !giveaway - View current giveaway information
  • !nextgiveaway - Check when the next auto giveaway starts
  • !lottery - View current lottery details
  • !lottery join <tickets> - Buy lottery tickets
  • !nextlottery - Check when the next auto lottery starts

Pro Tips

  • Both systems start at 00:00 UTC - set a reminder!
  • Winners are announced across ALL servers
  • Manual giveaways/lotteries can still be started by admins
  • Auto systems wait for manual ones to complete before starting

🔑 Character Keys System

A new way to unlock characters! Collect 750 keys for any character to unlock them directly.

How Character Keys Work

  • Each character has their own key type (e.g., "Nix Keys", "Bruce Keys")
  • Collect 750 keys to unlock a specific character
  • Keys only work for characters in your server's game bundle
  • Keys are permanent - they never expire!

Getting Character Keys

Key Rush Events: Special 1-hour events where ALL drops become character keys!

  • Main Server: Automatic at 10:00, 16:00, and 22:00 UTC daily (free!)
  • Other Servers: ZooAdmins activate with !keyrush (250 gems, 1 hour)
  • During Key Rush: Keys drop every 45-60 seconds

Normal Gameplay: 5% chance for keys to drop during regular gameplay (1-2 keys)

Character Keys Commands

  • !charkeys or !ck - View your key collection with progress bars
  • !keyunlock <character> - Unlock a character using 750 keys
  • !convertkeys - Convert excess keys to tokens (1:1 for owned characters)
  • !keyrushstatus - Check if Key Rush is active and time remaining
  • !keyrush - Activate Key Rush (ZooAdmin only, 250 gems, 1 hour)
  • !grantkeyrush [serverID] - Grant free Key Rush (Super Admin only)

Key Collection Strategy

  • Participate in all Key Rush events for maximum keys
  • Focus on collecting keys for characters you want
  • Excess keys for owned characters auto-convert to tokens
  • Check progress with !charkeys to see beautiful progress bars

⚔️ Battle System

Master the turn-based combat system to climb the trophy ranks!

Basic Mechanics

  • Battles are turn-based - each player takes turns attacking
  • Moves cost energy - manage it wisely!
  • Higher level characters have more HP and damage
  • Each character has unique passive abilities

Energy Management

You start with 100 energy. Basic attacks cost less, but special moves deal more damage. Plan your moves to finish strong!

Winning Strategies

  • Level up your main battler before challenging strong opponents
  • Learn your character's ability - some trigger on low HP, others on crits
  • Balance energy usage - don't burn all energy on one move
  • Study opponent patterns and counter them

🏰 Clan System

Join forces with your server and compete in weekly Clan Wars!

How Clans Work

  • Each server has one clan that all members can join
  • Donate coins, gems, or trophies to increase clan power

  • Top 3 clans receive UST rewards for all members!

UST Rewards

Use UST to buy exclusive skins and profile pictures that can't be obtained any other way!

❓ FAQ

How often do drops happen?

Main server: Every 20-30 seconds (FREE). Other servers: Every 20-30 seconds when drops are active (100 gems for 3 hours via !paydrops).

What's the best way to get rare characters?

1) Collect 750 character keys and use !keyunlock, 2) Open Tyrant Crates, 3) Participate in events, 4) Trade with other players, or 5) Get lucky with cage keys!

When do auto giveaways and lotteries start?

Both start automatically at 00:00 UTC every day and run for 60 minutes. Use !nextgiveaway or !nextlottery to check timing.

How do I level up faster?

Battle frequently, complete quests, and use coins to directly level up your characters with !levelup.

Can I trade between servers?

Trading happens within servers. Your collection is global, but trades must be with players in the same server.

How do ZooAdmins activate drops?

Server ZooAdmins use !paydrops which costs 100 gems for 3 hours of active drops.
